Interpretation and reproducibility

Donation adds net energy B−C; mitosis conserves parent-plus-child energy. Behavioural history counts donations given. Mimics cannot donate and are excluded from PPO. The simulator and earlier studies share one resident policy. The separately labelled inherited-policy study uses family networks and explicit selection. Rule baselines can all reach capacity: compare donation frequency and exploitation, not the population cap alone.
